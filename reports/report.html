Overview and description

This project involves creating a database to manage peoples skills and roles. Throught this project you’ll see displays of tables from the database a specific queries that display varying information regarding the tables below.

Crows-foot diagram

This is a diagram of 5 tables: SKILLS, PEOPLE, PEOPLE SKILLS, ROLES< PEOPLEROLES Since the relationship between SKILLS – PEOPLE and PEOPLE – ROLES, is many-to-many, it was resolved with PEOPLESKILLS and PEOPLEROLES. This means that one skill and or role can appear multiple times but each entry in PEOPLESKILLS/PEOPLEROLES refers to only one skill and or role . One person can have multiple skills and roles through PEOPLESKILLS/PEOPLEROLES but each entry in PEOPLESKILLS/PEOPLEROLES refers to one person.

List all people who have acquired more than 2 skills

(Goal: Join through multiple tables and use HAVING)

run_sql_and_return_html( cnx,f"""

from people p
